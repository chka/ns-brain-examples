## Returns

This section covers what customers can return, how long they have, what they get back, and when to hand a question to the store team. The rules are the same for store purchases and online orders unless a section says otherwise. Never promise anything beyond what is written here. If a case isn't covered, send it to the store team at ny@kestrelrow.example or (212) 555-0147.

### Quick reference

| Situation | Time limit | What the customer gets |
|---|---|---|
| Full-price item, with receipt or order number | 30 days from purchase (delivery for online orders) | Refund to the original payment method |
| Full-price item, no receipt | 30 days | Store credit at the lowest price in the last 30 days |
| Sale item | 14 days | Store credit or exchange only |
| Item marked FINAL SALE | None | No return, no exchange |
| Holiday purchase (Nov 1 to Dec 24) | Until Jan 15 | Full-price rules; sale items keep 14 days |
| Faulty item | Within 1 year | Repair, replacement or refund |
| Underwear, earrings, gift cards, altered items, swimwear without the hygiene liner | Not returnable | None |

### Full-price items

A full-price item can be returned within **30 days** of purchase. For online orders, the 30 days start at **delivery**.

The customer needs:
- The receipt or the order number.
- The item **unworn, with tags attached**.

The refund goes back to the **original payment method**. We do not swap the refund to another card, to cash, or to a gift card unless the store team agrees to it.

If the item is worn, the tags are missing, or it has been altered, it does not meet the policy. Be kind but clear, and offer to pass the case to the store team if the customer disagrees.

### Sale items and FINAL SALE items

**Sale items** can be returned within **14 days**. The customer receives **store credit or an exchange only**, never a refund to the card. This applies even with a receipt.

**Items marked FINAL SALE** cannot be returned or exchanged, with or without a receipt. Do not make exceptions. If a customer says an item was not marked FINAL SALE when they bought it, pass the case to the store team.

For online sale orders, the policy states the 14-day limit but does not say how the days are counted. If a customer is near the limit, send the question to the store team rather than guessing.

### Receipts and returns without a receipt

#### With a receipt or order number
This is the standard path. The customer gets the refund rules above, depending on whether the item was full price or sale.

#### Looking up a purchase instead of a receipt
A purchase made by **card** or with a **Row Rewards account** can be looked up in place of a paper receipt. Before treating a return as "no receipt," ask whether the customer paid by card or has a Row Rewards account. Never ask a customer to tell you a card number or a password. In store, the team looks the purchase up at the register. In chat, the assistant can only see what the shop holds about a logged-in customer. If it can't find the purchase, the store team handles the lookup.

#### True no-receipt returns
When there is no receipt and no way to look the purchase up:
- The customer receives **store credit**, not a refund.
- The credit is at the **lowest price the item sold for in the last 30 days**.
- **Photo ID is required.**
- Each customer is limited to **3 no-receipt returns per year**.

Explain this up front so the customer isn't surprised by the credit amount. The assistant cannot check ID or count a customer's no-receipt returns, so for these cases direct the customer to the store.

### Items that can never be returned

Some items cannot be returned under any circumstances:
- **Underwear**
- **Swimwear without the hygiene liner**
- **Earrings**
- **Gift cards**
- **Altered items**

Two points come up often:
- **Alterations include free hemming.** Hemming on full-price jeans and trousers is free, but once an item has been altered it can no longer be returned. Mention this when a customer books a hem, particularly if they are unsure about the fit.
- **Gift cards** are not returnable and are not redeemable for cash. A lost gift card can only be replaced with the purchase receipt.

- This extended window applies to **full-price** items, with the usual conditions (unworn, tags attached, receipt or order lookup).
- **Sale items keep their 14 days.**
- Items marked FINAL SALE, and the never-returnable items above, stay non-returnable.

Because the store is closed on Thanksgiving (Thursday, November 26) and on Christmas Day, customers can't bring items back on those days. Regular store hours otherwise apply to returns:

### Faulty items

If an item is faulty, the customer has **1 year** to come back to us. We offer a **repair, a replacement or a refund**.

- The policy doesn't say who decides between repair, replacement and refund, or how a fault is judged. Don't promise a particular outcome. Ask the customer to bring the item to the store, or contact the store team, who will assess it.
- Normal wear and tear and care mistakes aren't covered by this policy as written. If the question is whether a particular problem counts as a fault, the store team decides.

1. Is the item on the never-returnable list or marked FINAL SALE? If so, it can't be returned.

6. Is the issue a fault? Then the 1-year rule applies, and the store team assesses it.
7. Would a free same-item exchange solve the problem?

### Sales tax in New York City

New York City sales tax is **8.875%**. A big part of our range is clothing and footwear, and these have a special rule:

> **Clothing and footwear under $110 per item are exempt from sales tax in New York City.**

The key points are:

- The $110 threshold applies **per item**, not to the total of the basket.
- An item priced **under $110** is exempt.
- An item priced at **$110 or more** is not covered by the exemption, so the 8.875% rate applies.
- The register calculates tax at the point of sale. Customers don't need to do anything.

#### How the exemption applies across our lines

Use this table to orient yourself. It shows each line's price range against the $110 threshold, based on the list prices.

For lines marked "Depends on the item," check the actual price of the specific piece before telling a customer whether tax applies. If you are unsure, say the register will show the exact tax on the receipt.

### Visitors and tax-free shopping

Customers visiting from abroad often ask about tax refunds.

- **Tax-free shopping for visitors is not available.**
- The US has no VAT refund, so there is nothing to claim at the airport or at the store.
- The clothing exemption described above is not a visitor scheme. It applies to everyone, on every qualifying item under $110.

Be friendly and direct. Don't suggest a workaround, and don't refer customers to any outside refund service.

### Helping a customer choose a size

Work through these steps in order. Keep answers short.

1. **Find the line.** Ask which piece they have in mind, or which line it belongs to.
2. **Give the fit note.** Use the table above. If the line has no fit note, say so honestly and move to step 4.
3. **Name the adjustment.** For example, for Harbour: "Harbour jeans run small, so we suggest going up one size from your usual." For Burren: "Burren boots run large, so we suggest going down one size."
4. **Offer a way to check.** The options are a fitting room, a free styling appointment, or a stock check.
5. **Remind them of the safety net.** Exchanges for a different size are free within 30 days (14 days for sale items), in store or online.

#### Common situations

**The customer is between two sizes.** Use the fit note first. If the line runs small (Harbour), the larger size is the safer suggestion. If it runs large (Burren), the smaller size. For lines with no note, suggest trying both in store.

**The customer is over 6 ft and wants a Corrib shirt.** Recommend Regular, not Slim.

**The customer likes a snug knit.** Explain that Atlantic is true to size but roomy in the body, so they may want to try their usual size and see how it sits.

**The customer is buying a gift and does not know the size.** Suggest a Kestrel Row gift card ($25 to $500, never expires, free gift box for plastic cards in store) or point out that exchanges are free. Gift wrapping is free at the register.

**The customer wants measurements.** We do not give measurements in this handbook. Direct them to the store team.
