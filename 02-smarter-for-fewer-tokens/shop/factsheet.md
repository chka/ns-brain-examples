# Kestrel Row New York: fact sheet (source of truth for the handbook)

Kestrel Row is an invented clothing shop: its own label of men's, women's and kids' clothing, sold in one
store in SoHo, New York, and online across the US. Prices are in US dollars.

## The store
- Address: 147 Greene Street, between Prince and Spring Streets, SoHo, New York, NY 10012.
- Subway: Prince St (R, W) 2 minutes, Spring St (C, E) 4 minutes, Spring St (6) 6 minutes, Broadway-Lafayette
  (B, D, F, M) 6 minutes.
- No store parking. Nearest garage: 110 Mercer Street, about $28 for two hours. Citi Bike dock on Prince St at Greene.
- Step-free entrance, elevator to the lower floor, 8 fitting rooms (one accessible). Dogs on a leash welcome.
  Customer restroom on the lower floor. Free Wi-Fi.
- Phone (212) 555-0147, email ny@kestrelrow.example. Phone answered during store hours.

## Hours
- Monday to Wednesday 10am-8pm. Thursday 10am-9pm (late night). Friday and Saturday 10am-8pm. Sunday 11am-7pm.
- Holiday hours 2026: Thanksgiving, Thursday Nov 26: CLOSED. Black Friday, Nov 27: 8am-10pm. Saturday Nov 28:
  9am-9pm. Sunday Nov 29: 10am-8pm. Christmas Eve: 10am-5pm. Christmas Day: closed. New Year's Eve: 10am-6pm.
  New Year's Day: 12pm-6pm.
- Last fitting room entry 15 minutes before closing.

## Returns (in store and online orders)
- Full-price items: 30 days from purchase (or delivery for online orders) with the receipt or order number,
  unworn with tags. Refund to the original payment method.
- No receipt: store credit at the lowest price in the last 30 days, photo ID required, 3 no-receipt returns a year.
  A purchase made by card or with a Row Rewards account can be looked up instead of a receipt.
- Sale items: 14 days, store credit or exchange only. Items marked FINAL SALE: no returns or exchanges.
- Never returnable: underwear, swimwear without the hygiene liner, earrings, gift cards, altered items.
- Holiday returns: purchases from Nov 1 to Dec 24 can be returned until Jan 15 (full price; sale items keep 14 days).
- Online orders can be returned in store or by mail with the prepaid label in the box ($7 taken off the refund;
  free for Row Rewards members).
- Refunds: card refunds are processed at once in store; banks take 3-10 business days. Online returns by mail:
  refunded within 5 business days of arriving.
- Faulty items: within 1 year, repair, replacement or refund.

## Exchanges
- Same item, different size or color: within 30 days (14 for sale items), in store or online. Free.

## Payment and tax
- Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover, Apple Pay, Google Pay, cash, Kestrel Row gift cards, Klarna (pay in 4,
  $50 to $1,000, via the Klarna app at the register or online). No Venmo, no checks, no crypto.
- NYC sales tax 8.875%. Clothing and footwear under $110 per item are exempt from sales tax in New York City.
- Tax-free shopping for visitors: not available (US has no VAT refund).

## Gift cards
- $25 to $500, plastic in store (free gift box) or e-gift online. Never expire. Balance at the register, on the
  website or by phone. Not redeemable for cash. Lost cards replaced only with the purchase receipt.

## Alterations and services
- Free hemming on full-price jeans and trousers bought at Kestrel Row. Other alterations from $15 (sleeves $25,
  taking in a waist $20). Ready in 5 business days; rush in 48 hours for $15 extra. Pick up at the alterations
  counter, lower floor, during store hours. Altered items cannot be returned.
- Free gift wrapping at the register. Free personal styling appointments (45 minutes), book online or by phone.
- Items can be held for 24 hours by phone or chat. Stock check by chat or phone.

## Online orders
- Free standard shipping (3-5 business days) over $100, otherwise $8. Express 2 business days $18. Same-day courier
  in Manhattan $25 when ordered by 1pm. Ships to all 50 states; no international shipping.
- Buy online, pick up in store: free, ready in 2 hours during store hours, held for 7 days.
- Orders ship the same business day if placed by 2pm Eastern.
- An order can be changed or cancelled within 60 minutes.

## Row Rewards
- Free. 1 point per $1 spent. 200 points = $10 reward. Points expire 12 months after earning. 15% birthday code.
  Free return shipping for members. Points are removed for returned items.

## Discounts and prices
- Students 10% with a valid student ID, not on sale items. Military, veterans and first responders 15% with ID.
- One discount per purchase. No price matching. Price adjustment once within 7 days if the same item is marked down.
- Black Friday weekend (Fri Nov 27 to Mon Nov 30): 25% off full-price items in store and online. Not with other discounts.

## Product lines
- Harbour (women's jeans, $98-128), Corrib (men's shirts, $68-88), Atlantic (merino knitwear, $118-178), Maam
  (waxed jackets, $245-310), Inis (linen, $58-148), Claddagh (dresses, $110-190), Burren (leather boots, $210-260),
  Puffin (kids, $22-75).
- Fit notes: Harbour jeans run small, size up one. Corrib Slim runs slim; above 6 ft choose Regular. Burren boots run
  large, size down one. Claddagh true to size. Atlantic true to size, roomy in the body.
- Women's sizes 0-16 (US). Women's jeans waist 24-34. Men's tops XS-XXL. Men's trousers waist 28-40. Kids 2-12.
  Shoes US women 5-11, US men 7-13.

## Care
- Merino: hand wash or wool cycle cold, dry flat, no dryer. Linen: machine wash warm, line dry. Denim: wash inside
  out cold, no dryer. Waxed cotton: never machine wash, sponge with cold water, re-wax yearly (free re-waxing in store,
  48 hours). Leather boots: brush, condition every 3 months.
